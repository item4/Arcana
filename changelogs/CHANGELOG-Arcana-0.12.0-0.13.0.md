# Changelog

# 📦 0.13.0

## 📰 General changes and notes

**BACKUP FIRST**

0.13.0 closes a long backlog of reported issues. Observable has been added and Carry On has been removed. Read the previous changelogs if you're not on 0.12.0 yet.

NeoForge version is 21.1.248


<details open>
<summary>Pack changes :octocat:</summary>
<blockquote>

- The Alchemist Cauldron accepts EvilCraft blood, for emptying Blood Vials and soaking Bloody Vellum (#211) (item4)
- Apotheosis quest changes, including new Ancient Reforging and Gateways quests (#212) (DivineFinal)
- Reverted the Applied Energistics 2 questline to its pre-0.12.0 state (#213) (LobsterJonn)
- The ATM Bee's Star Shards can now be crafted into an ATM Star (#199) (LobsterJonn)
- Dark Gem Dust can now be made by using an AllTheOres ore hammer on a Dark Gem (#63) (LobsterJonn)
- Wither Bones from Baubley Heart Canisters and Ice And Fire are now interchangeable (#124) (LobsterJonn)
- The Rod of Lyssa from Reliquary Reincarnations can no longer steal from players, villagers, wandering traders or any of 34 bosses, and now costs an Allthemodium Rod instead of a fishing rod (#176) (LobsterJonn)
- The Omega Tier Stack Upgrade from Sophisticated Backpacks and Sophisticated Storage is no longer craftable, matching the other All the Mods packs (#21) (LobsterJonn)
- Tamed Ice And Fire dragons no longer break blocks (#197) (LobsterJonn)
- Disabled Dragon Mounts Remastered converting the vanilla dragon egg (#58) (LobsterJonn)
- Disabled Vampirism's point-of-interest protection in faction-owned villages (#201) (LobsterJonn)
- The Ender Dragon and the Wither are now tagged as bosses (LobsterJonn)
- Blocked the Protection of Ash effect from Ender's Spells and Stuff: Requiem (LobsterJonn)
- Suppressed Apotheosis log spam (#147) (LobsterJonn)
- Added Halcyon player data migration script (LobsterJonn)

</blockquote>
